Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

In short: CRT Sync Camera does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any information to us or to third parties.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera to show the live preview, measure your CRT's refresh rate and record photos and video. The microphone records audio for your videos. All of this is processed on your device and is never uploaded.

Photos

The app asks for permission to add photos and videos to your library so it can save what you capture. It does not read or browse your existing library.

Settings

Your preferences (such as adjustments, recording format and the band position you prefer) are stored only on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our support page.
