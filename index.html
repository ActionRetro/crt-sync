Film CRT screens without the rolling bar.

CRT Sync Camera is an iPhone camera made for filming CRT monitors and TVs. It measures your screen's exact refresh rate, syncs the shutter to it, and parks the leftover band just off-screen so your footage looks clean.

  • Detect measures any CRT's refresh rate in about 15 seconds.
  • Band parking hides the residual band at the top edge, and learns your CRT's drift so it stays there.
  • Moiré reducer, CRT looks and adjustments for preview, video and photos.
  • Pro camera controls: manual focus with peaking, white balance, EV, 4K HEVC, H.264 and ProRes.
