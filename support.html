Getting started

For a full walkthrough, see the User Guide.

  1. Point your iPhone at the CRT and tap Detect.
  2. Fit the screen inside the green box and hold still for about 15 seconds. The app measures the refresh rate, syncs the shutter and parks the band at the top edge.
  3. If you'd like the band somewhere else, drag the Band slider. The app remembers where you like it.
  4. If the band slowly drifts back into view, drag it back once or twice. The app learns your CRT's drift from each correction.

Common questions

Why does the frame rate read 33.32 fps instead of 29.97?

To hold the band still, the app runs the camera at an exact fraction of your CRT's refresh rate. Set your editing timeline to your normal frame rate (for example 29.97) and the clip will be conformed automatically.

Why can't the band be hidden completely?

iPhone shutter speeds come in fixed steps, so most CRTs can't be matched exactly. The leftover band is a thin, slightly tilted line; on some screens a small sliver stays visible in a corner. Drag the Band slider to tuck it wherever it's least noticeable.

How do I reduce moiré?

Open Adjustments. Lens soften defocuses very slightly (tap the screen to focus first). Color moiré removes rainbow patterns. Moving a little closer or farther, or tilting the phone a few degrees, also helps.

Which recording format should I use?

HEVC gives the smallest files, H.264 plays everywhere, and ProRes 422 is best for editing but uses several gigabytes per minute in 4K.

Subscriptions

  • Manage or cancel: Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or the Camera panel › Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.
  • Restore: tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in the Camera panel, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Works with CRT monitors and TVs from about 45 to 130 Hz (NTSC, PAL, VGA, Mac and more).
